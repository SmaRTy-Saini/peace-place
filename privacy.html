Privacy policy

This website is designed to collect only the information needed to respond to project inquiries.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, work email, organization, project category, and message. Do not submit protected health information, patient identifiers, passwords, API keys, or other sensitive credentials through the public website.

Form processing

Because this website is statically hosted, the public contact form uses a third-party form relay to deliver inquiries by email. Sensitive technical or healthcare information should be moved to an agreed secure channel after initial contact.

Hosting and logs

The website is hosted as static content. The hosting provider may process standard technical request information such as IP address, browser information, and access logs according to its own infrastructure policies.

Case studies

Public project examples are anonymized and are written to avoid client identities, patient information, confidential credentials, or implementation secrets.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@smartysaini.com.
