Privacy Policy

How SmaRTy Saini handles information submitted through this website.

Website inquiries

Information you submit through the contact form is used to respond to your inquiry and evaluate a potential engagement. Do not submit protected health information or sensitive credentials through the public form.

Form delivery

This static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Contact-form submissions are relayed through a third-party form service to contact@smartysaini.com. If your inquiry requires sensitive information, establish an approved secure communication method first.

Analytics and cookies

This version of the site does not intentionally deploy advertising trackers. Hosting and network providers may maintain technical logs required to deliver and secure their services.

Healthcare information

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, accession numbers, results, or other protected health information in the public website form.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@smartysaini.com.
