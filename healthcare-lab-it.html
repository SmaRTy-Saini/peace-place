Laboratories are where application support, infrastructure, interfaces, reporting and operations collide. I work across the whole path: orders, results, analyzers, pending work, custom reports, documents, secure delivery, data extracts and the infrastructure underneath it.

Custom reporting is operational software

A laboratory report is rarely “just a report.” It may determine what staff work next, what gets escalated, what gets released, what gets delivered to a client, and what can be proven later. I build reports around the operational decision they need to support.

Interfaces are only part of the workflow

An order can arrive successfully and still create manual work because codes, insurance, patient identifiers, client rules or report-delivery logic do not line up. I work on the surrounding normalization, validation and exception-handling layers as well.

The PDF and document layer matters

Laboratories generate and move large numbers of reports and attachments. Work has included automated document processing, secure PDF delivery, client-specific suppression rules, report generation and evidence that a document was processed or delivered.

Production support requires understanding dependencies

When results stop moving, the failure can be in the source system, interface engine, network, file path, database, report generator, API, destination or acknowledgment. Troubleshooting follows the entire chain instead of assuming the application vendor is the only place to look.

Common questions
