Common questions

Before you reach out

Are you an MSP, an integration consultant, or a software developer?

All three capabilities are available, but the business model is simpler: one senior technical partner who can own support and infrastructure, then go deeper into security, interoperability, data, reporting, automation and custom software when the problem requires it.

Can you take over from an existing IT provider or internal team?

Yes. I can fully own the environment, provide senior escalation and project capacity alongside an internal team, or handle the specialized work a general provider cannot cover.

Do you work outside healthcare?

Yes. Healthcare and laboratory systems are a deep specialty because that is where much of my production experience comes from. Managed IT, networking, cybersecurity, cloud, automation and software work apply to businesses in many industries.

How do projects get priced?

Defined projects are scoped and quoted in writing before work starts. Ongoing IT and support can be structured as a monthly engagement. The goal is predictable ownership, not surprise hourly billing.

Do you sign BAAs for healthcare work?

Yes. When an engagement requires access to protected health information, the appropriate agreement and access controls are established before that access begins.
