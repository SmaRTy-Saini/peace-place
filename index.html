FAQ

Where does SmaRTy Saini fit?

Are you a managed IT provider or a consultant?

Both models are available. I can provide ongoing Tier 1–3 technical support and ownership, or step in for a specific project, rescue, modernization, integration, security, or automation engagement.

Can you support normal IT issues too?

Yes. The value of the model is that routine user/device issues and deeper infrastructure or engineering problems can stay under the same technical ownership instead of being split across unrelated providers.

Is healthcare the only industry you work with?

No. Healthcare and laboratory technology are deep specialties, but the broader work—networking, cloud, security, support, automation, infrastructure, databases, and custom software—applies to many organizations.

Can you work alongside an internal IT team or existing vendor?

Yes. I can fill gaps, own a specific layer, take escalation work, lead projects, or handle the cross-system issues that existing teams and vendors are struggling to resolve.
